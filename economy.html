Economy of Belleville

Belleville also has a diverse economy that is worth around $5.7 billion. Some of the main industries in the area include advanced manufacturing, food processing, and logistics. These industries provide jobs for residents and help support the city's economy. Belleville's location also makes it useful for businesses because of its transportation connections and its position between Toronto and Ottawa meaning a strong connection between groups, allowing better trades and marketing.
